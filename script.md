# Garage Sale: demo script (3 minutes, one presenter)

**One-liner:** Garage Sale: snap a photo, and Grok Bot agents price it, list it on Facebook Marketplace, and line up pickups for you, while they hunt underpriced deals you can flip.

**Track:** The one person company. Bots run sales and support for a solo seller.

## Before judges arrive (8:20)
- Start a demo sell job on the phone so it is at "Your approval" when judges walk up.
- Laptop tabs: 1) the demo job page, 2) the DT 770 live Facebook listing, 3) the Dashboard, 4) Grok Bot's computer view.

## 0:00 Hook (15s)
"Everyone has stuff to sell. Listing it takes 20 minutes: photos, pricing, writing, answering buyers. Garage Sale does all of it with Grok Bot agents. Tonight it already posted real listings from my account."

## 0:15 Sell, live (75s)
1. Judge picks an object. Snap it on the phone.
2. Laptop: the agent log streams live. Identification, then Austin Marketplace comps, then eBay and Amazon if needed, then a recommended price.
3. While it researches, switch to the pre-staged job: "Here's one that already finished." Show comps, price range, and the drafted listing.
4. Tap **Approve & post**. Grok Bot's own computer opens Facebook and fills in the form: photos, title, price, category, condition, description, Austin location.
5. Show proof: the live DT 770 listing on Facebook. QR code so the judge opens it on their own phone.

## 1:30 Buy and flip (45s)
"Tell it what you want, or let it scout every 30 minutes." Show a finished buy job: real Marketplace links, buy price, where to resell, and the spread. Example: Aeron size B for $340, about $110 under a $450 used listing.

## 2:15 Dashboard (20s)
Money on the table: your items with recommended actions, the deals with profit chips. Like a FIFA transfer market for your garage.

## 2:35 How it uses Grok Bot (20s)
- **Computer + browser:** posts from the user's own signed-in Facebook. There's no public listing API.
- **Routines:** a webhook routine wakes the worker on every job, plus a 30-minute deal scout.
- **Approval-gated:** nothing posts and no buyer gets a reply without the user's tap.

## 2:55 Close (5s)
"Real photo, real price, real listing, in minutes. Garage Sale."

## If something breaks
Show the finished DT 770 and Razer jobs and their live listings. Never wait in silence: narrate the agent log.

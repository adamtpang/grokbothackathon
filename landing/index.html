How selling works

Five steps.
One button.

You take the photo and press A. The bots in town do everything in between.

01

Snap

Take one photo of the thing you want gone. The Workshop bot identifies exactly what it is.

02

Research

Local comps first. If there are fewer than 3, it widens to eBay sold, Amazon and other platforms for a true market range.

03

Write

The Print Shop drafts a detailed listing: title, condition, specs, and a price grounded in the comps.

04

Approve

Review it at Town Hall and press A on a real Game Boy. Nothing posts without you.

05

Live

Posted to Facebook Marketplace. Scan the QR code to see the real listing for yourself.

Buy

Deals find you.

Scout bots watch Facebook Marketplace in Austin, compare each listing against eBay sold, Amazon and other platforms, and surface the ones priced under market, with the comps to prove it.
